## Design Specificity Verdict

**LLM assessment.** Authored for this product. The four-hop request path with an honestly stateless hop 1, the What to check commands, the remove dialog's ordered steps and the add page's "Will be written" panel can belong to no other app. The copy states mechanism and keeps automated and manual apart. The generic part is the board and list row shape (pill, name, meta line). The weakest expression of product character is the retired page, which repeats a ~1,700px setup runbook nobody will act on.

## Overall Impression

A real step up. The 2026-10-21 problems (mono sentences, emphasis, retired invisible, the duplicate error) are gone, and the two heuristics that scored 2 then are both 3 now. What's left is about the read job under stress: the glance surfaces (rail counts, board order) don't yet tell the truth fast enough, and the long pages bury short states. The single biggest opportunity is making the rail and board honest and attention-first.

## What's Working

1. **The request path plus "What to check".** Tone lands on the one broken hop, with a Scorch key and a runnable command beside it. It is the product's promise as UI.
2. **The safety ordering is the interface.** The remove dialog numbers its steps, says a failure stops the rest, focuses Cancel and names the host on the destructive button. The add page shows what it will write before writing it.
3. **Live, specific validation from one shared validator.** For example "Port 4000 is already used by api.lychee.land."

## Priority Issues

**[P1] The rail's attention counts hide `unknown`.**
- **What:** `reconciler 0` appears while `lychee-watch-timer` is UNKNOWN. `services 3` doesn't count four unknown rows. The rule is `attention: tone === "bad" || trouble !== null` (railModel.ts:37).
- **Why it matters:** A neutral pill is right, but a numeric 0 is a claim of health. A stopped or unreadable watcher looks fine at 11pm.
- **Fix:** When a group holds an `unknown` unit, show it, for example `0 · 1 unknown` or a Smoke `?`. Keep it neutral, never Scorch.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify

**[P1] The board doesn't lead with trouble.**
- **What:** /services lists 11 rows in inventory order, with deploy trouble (swee, swee-staging, palsave-api, broken) interleaved among healthy rows.
- **Why it matters:** The board is where a wedged deploy surfaces, and the read job is equal to the mutate job.
- **Fix:** Attention rows first, or a one-line summary under the eyebrow linking to `#service-<name>`.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable layout

**[P2] Retired and unattached pages bury their state under setup.**
- **What:** gone.lychee.land is 3,359px. Its whole meaning is the notice and Prune, yet it inlines the full scaffold runbook, with an Attach the page itself says is refused. The detector's 13 em-dashes on that page agree.
- **Fix:** For a retired site, fold or drop the runbook ("Prune, then attach again"). Consider whether Manual steps (DNS) should fold once attached.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable distill

**[P2] Hostname errors: placement, wording, direction.**
- **What:**
  - Hostname rejections appear only in the panel's box, with no `aria-invalid` and no field-level error (AddSiteForm.tsx:64).
  - `"Shop!!" must be a subdomain of lychee.land` misstates invalid characters (siteValidation.ts:92).
  - #123's new "Fix the port **above**" is wrong at desktop width, where the port field is to the panel's left (AddSitePage.tsx:233).
- **Fix:**
  - Put an inline hostname error under the field, as the port has.
  - Split the message into invalid characters and wrong domain.
  - Say "in the form" rather than "above".
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable harden

**[P2] A blocked preview lists a step that won't run.**
- **What:** With reverse proxy chosen, "In this order" shows "3. Site directory created" unmarked until a preview succeeds.
- **Why it matters:** It brushes Principle 2: nothing may imply a step runs when it won't.
- **Fix:** Mark it skipped from the type alone, using `STEP_MARKS.skipped`.
- **Suggested command:** /impeccable clarify
